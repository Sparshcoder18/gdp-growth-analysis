Define Annual GDP Growth Function¶

Compute year-over-year GDP growth for a selected year relative to the previous year.

def compute_growth(df, year):
    """
    Computes GDP growth (%) for a given year relative to previous year.
    Returns a pandas Series.
    """
    if year - 1 not in df.columns:
        raise ValueError("Previous year not available in dataset.")
    
    growth = ((df[year] - df[year - 1]) / df[year - 1]) * 100
    return growth

Annual Growth Formula:

Growth(t) = ((GDP(t) - GDP(t-1)) / GDP(t-1)) x 100

Compute total percentage growth between any two selected years.

def compute_growth_between(df, start_year, end_year):
    """
    Computes total percentage growth between two selected years.
    """
    if start_year not in df.columns or end_year not in df.columns:
        raise ValueError("Selected years not found in dataset.")
    
    growth = ((df[end_year] - df[start_year]) / df[start_year]) * 100
    return growth

Define CAGR Function¶

This measure captures the constant annualized growth rate required to move from the starting GDP level to the ending GDP level over the selected time horizon.

CAGR (Compound Annual Growth Rate) measures the constant annual growth rate that would take GDP from its starting value to its ending value over a specified period. It is especially useful for long-run comparisons because it smooths irregular year-to-year variation into a single interpretable rate.

In [36]:
def compute_cagr(df, start_year, end_year):
    """
    Computes CAGR (%) between two years.
    """
    n_years = end_year - start_year
    
    cagr = ((df[end_year] / df[start_year]) ** (1 / n_years) - 1) * 100
    return cagr

Growth Volatility¶

Growth volatility is defined as the standard deviation of annual GDP growth rates across the analysis period. It serves as a simple indicator of macroeconomic stability.

def compute_growth_volatility(df, years):
    growth_rates = []
    
    for year in years[1:]:
        growth = ((df[year] - df[year - 1]) / df[year - 1]) * 100
        growth_rates.append(growth)
    
    growth_df = pd.concat(growth_rates, axis=1)

def prep_indicator(source_df, indicator_name, prefix, analysis_years):
    out = source_df[source_df["IndicatorName"] == indicator_name].copy()
    out[analysis_years] = out[analysis_years].apply(pd.to_numeric, errors="coerce") / 1e9
    out = out[out[analysis_years].notna().all(axis=1)].copy()
    out = out[["Country"] + analysis_years].set_index("Country")
    out.columns = [f"{prefix}_{y}" for y in analysis_years]
    return out
